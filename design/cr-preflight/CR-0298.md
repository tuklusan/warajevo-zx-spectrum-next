## Change Request
CR-0298: Reuse complete matrix evidence for post-matrix instrumentation repairs

Status: APPROVED_FOR_IMPLEMENTATION
Priority: medium

## Functional Inventory
- Distinguish a matrix product/build/test failure from a failure in post-matrix instrumentation or evidence handling.
- Preserve the last complete matrix run and its exact-commit evidence when the matrix itself reached terminal state and passed its acceptance criteria.
- Permit correction of manifests, log forwarding, screenshot/trace inspection, or publication instrumentation without rerunning product build/test lanes when the correction cannot affect product execution.
- Require a fresh matrix run whenever product source, tests, build configuration, runner selection, or lane execution semantics change.
- Record the reused run ID, exact commit, correction scope, and reason in the active CR closure record.
- Keep queued or incomplete runs ineligible for reuse.
- Keep the existing single-run, fail-fast-disabled, terminal-state and publication gates unchanged for ordinary product failures.

## Upstream Discovery
Primary authorities are `WORKFLOW.md`, `tools/harness/README.md`,
`tools/validate_project_gates.py`, and `.github/workflows/platform-smoke.yml`.
The architecture documents do not define CI evidence reuse behavior. Upstream
Warajevo was searched for matrix, publication, manifest, and instrumentation
concepts; no upstream CI behavior governs this repository workflow.

## Disposition
`IMPLEMENT_NOW`: add the explicit evidence-reuse rule to the canonical workflow
and harness guidance, and require the rule in the repository gate. No harness,
wrapper, product build, or test behavior is changed.

## Zero-Gap Exit Scan
Confirm the rule covers complete versus incomplete runs, product versus
instrumentation changes, exact-commit binding, retained evidence, closure
records, and the mandatory fresh-run cases. Confirm all three public rule
surfaces agree and no working harness is modified.
