# CR-0194 Implement 128K Banked Memory and Paging Latch

Status: CLOSED

## Functional Inventory

- Add eight independent 16 KiB RAM banks for the base 128K PAL profile.
- Map bank 5 at `0x4000..0x7fff`, bank 2 at `0x8000..0xbfff`, and the
  `0x7ffd`-selected bank at `0xc000..0xffff`.
- Implement the base 128K `0x7ffd` partial address decode and write-only latch.
- Implement RAM-page, screen-select, ROM-select, and paging-disable latch bits,
  preserving the latch after bit 5 disables later writes until reset.
- Keep the validated 48K flat-memory and ROM behavior unchanged.
- Keep +2A/+3 special paging, 128K contention, ULA differences, and AY behavior
  outside this CR and mapped to their ordered later tasks.
- Add headless deterministic mapping/latch regression coverage.

## Upstream Completeness Gate

Rescan the preserved upstream memory/paging and machine-code paths for partial decode,
selected bank mapping, screen selection, ROM selection, write-only behavior, and the
paging-disable latch. Compare every behavior with the 128K evidence freeze and reject
or defer only explicitly out-of-profile variants.

## Upstream Discovery

The upstream reference maps bank 5 at `0x4000`, bank 2 at `0x8000`, and one of banks
0-7 at `0xc000`; `0x7ffd` bits 0-2 select the latter bank, bit 3 selects the displayed
screen, bit 4 selects the ROM, and bit 5 disables future paging writes until reset.
The port is partially decoded for addresses with bits 1 and 15 clear.

## Disposition

- Base 128K eight-bank RAM and `0x7ffd` latch: `IMPLEMENT_NOW`.
- 48K compatibility behavior: `PRESERVE_EXISTING_CONTRACT`.
- Contention and paging timing: `DEFERRED` to Tasks 185-187.
- +2A/+3 ports, special paging modes, and extra ROM variants: `CONTROLLED_ERROR` or
  later explicitly scoped CR; do not silently implement them here.
- Serialization of full 128K live state: `DEFERRED` to the Phase-9 state tasks.
