# CR-0053 Migration Preflight

Status: APPROVED_FOR_IMPLEMENTATION

## Functional Inventory

- Implement `DAA` adjustment for all accumulator, carry, half-carry, and
  subtract-state combinations.
- Implement `SCF` and `CCF` flag preservation and undocumented X/Y flag
  sourcing.
- Preserve exact primary instruction timing and PC/R state progression.
- Verify all pinned Fuse vectors for opcodes `0x27`, `0x37`, and `0x3f`.

## Upstream Discovery

Required reference: `upstream/main`, `src/spectrum-kernel/Z80.ASM`, including
the `DAA_`, `SCF`, and `CCF` dispatch targets and their flag helpers. The
second scan established the following complete inventory:

- `DAA_` delegates addition and subtraction decimal adjustment to the
  processor adjustment helpers. Both H/C state and the original accumulator
  nibbles govern decimal correction; output S/Z/PV/X/Y derive from the result,
  N is retained, H derives from the adjustment transition, and C records the
  high decimal correction.
- `SCF` clears H/N, sets C, and preserves S/Z/PV/X/Y from the prior F register.
- `CCF` clears N, moves prior C into H, complements C, and preserves S/Z/PV/X/Y
  from the prior F register.
- The pinned Fuse vectors `27_1`, `37_1`, and `3f` confirm the otherwise
  undocumented subtraction and X/Y cases. All three retain their ordinary
  primary instruction fetch timing, PC increment, and R increment.

## Disposition

| Functional aspect | Disposition | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| DAA addition and subtraction adjustment | IMPLEMENT_NOW | Upstream `DAA_` and pinned Fuse vectors |
| SCF/CCF preserved flag bits | IMPLEMENT_NOW | Upstream handlers and pinned Fuse vectors |
| Primary fetch timing and PC/R progression | RETAINED_EXISTING | Existing primary execution and Fuse bus records |
| Prefix, I/O, and other flag instructions | FOLLOW_ON_CR | Separate dispatch families outside this opcode slice |

## Zero-Gap Exit Scan

Re-scan the upstream dispatch targets and all affected primary opcode-table
entries after implementation. The CR may close only when the pinned Fuse
vectors for these opcodes pass on all approved remote platforms. The post-edit
scan must confirm no other primary dispatch target shares these flag helpers.
