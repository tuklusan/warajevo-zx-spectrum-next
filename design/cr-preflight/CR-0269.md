# CR-0269 Forward Secured Lab and Hosted Harness Diagnostics

Status: OPEN
Priority: MEDIUM
Execution order: AFTER_WORKFLOW_CRS
Blocking: NO

## Functional Inventory

- Forward complete runner-matrix and local-lab harness diagnostics over UDP
  only to `sanyalnet-oracle-vps2.duckdns.org:65514`.
- Encode records in the project-approved Unix syslog format.
- Include enough run, lane, machine, phase, and failure classification data to
  diagnose queue, SSH, build, test, transport, and publication problems.
- Preserve non-blocking, best-effort behavior and never change product or test
  execution because the endpoint is unavailable.
- Forward secrets, private binaries, screenshots, and raw test artifacts when
  the operator has explicitly authorized the secured lab endpoint. Preserve
  only the transport credential itself as a local secret; it must not be
  embedded in source or packet payloads.
- Do not forward unrelated personal data or unreviewed machine data.
- Keep local evidence retention and the secured network forwarding path
  separate and auditable.
