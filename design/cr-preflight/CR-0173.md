# CR-0173: Conservative Tape Trap Eligibility

Status: APPROVED_FOR_IMPLEMENTATION

## Objective

Add an explicit, machine-readable eligibility boundary for Instant/Trap tape
loading. Eligibility must be positive and conservative. No uncertain or
unrecognized loader path may be treated as eligible, and this ticket must not
mutate machine state or perform an accelerated load.

## Functional Inventory

- Report why an Instant/Trap request is not eligible.
- Distinguish Normal mode, absent tape, absent ROM, and no recognized loader.
- Permit eligibility only after all prerequisites and positive loader
  recognition exist.
- Preserve the current machine on every eligibility query.
- Keep custom loaders, copy protection, unusual pulse encodings,
  timing-dependent software, unsupported ROM variants, and incomplete loader
  recognition on the safe Normal path.
- Keep actual accelerated state mutation and equivalence behavior in later
  tickets.

## Upstream Discovery

The preserved reference implementation was inspected in
`reference/original-warajevo/source/src/spectrum-kernel/TAPE.ASM` and
`reference/original-warajevo/source/src/timex-kernel/TSTAPE.ASM`. The sources
contain historical cassette sampling and `LOAD_MODE` state, but do not define a
portable WZSN trap-eligibility contract. The architecture's Sections 23.9 and
23.10 therefore govern the new boundary: positive recognition is required and
uncertainty falls back to Normal.

The current WZSN core has timed tape parsing/playback and explicit loading-mode
state, but no loader-recognition or trap-mutation implementation. Consequently
the implementation reports `NO_RECOGNIZED_LOADER` after the basic prerequisites
are satisfied rather than guessing from tape format alone.

## Disposition

Implemented here:

- Stable eligibility-reason enum and machine query API.
- Safe reasons for Normal mode, no tape, no ROM, and no recognized loader.
- Deterministic no-false-positive behavior and regression coverage.

Deferred by design:

- Positive ROM-loader recognition and supported-path metadata.
- Instant-load state mutation and safe fallback: Task 158.
- Post-load equivalence and error regressions: Tasks 159 and 166.

## Zero-Gap Exit Scan

The upstream and architecture scan was repeated after implementation. Every
known legacy loading concern is either represented by a conservative reason or
explicitly deferred to the loader and equivalence tickets. No format-only or
uncertain path is promoted to Instant/Trap eligibility.
